# Community code of conduct

## Our standard

Participate with respect, patience, and technical honesty. Welcome people with
different experience levels, criticize ideas rather than people, disclose
conflicts of interest, and avoid pressuring maintainers or contributors.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of
private information, credential disclosure, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[contact@certleap.net](mailto:contact@certleap.net?subject=AcmeMux%20conduct%20report).
Reports will be reviewed confidentially to the extent practical. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or ban an
account when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, and
other spaces where someone represents the AcmeMux project.
