# Sponsor AcmeMux

AcmeMux is independent open-source work. Sponsorship helps pay for release
engineering, security review, compatibility qualification, provider upkeep,
documentation, infrastructure, and maintainer time.

## Ways to sponsor

- **General support:** unrestricted one-time or recurring support with no
  roadmap promise.
- **Sustaining organization:** ongoing support with optional name or logo
  recognition and a periodic maintainer update.
- **Roadmap sponsorship:** funding for an existing accepted roadmap item.
- **Feature sponsorship:** a scoped proposal reviewed for fit and feasibility
  before an implementation commitment is accepted.

Self-service GitHub Sponsors payments are not enabled yet. Organizations that
want to fund the project now can request a scoped sponsorship or invoice by
emailing [sgurden@certleap.net](mailto:sgurden@certleap.net?subject=AcmeMux%20sponsorship).

For an already accepted roadmap proposal, a contribution of at least $500
guarantees placement in the sponsor-funded queue ahead of community-voted and
maintainer-selected feature work. It does not guarantee that $500 funds the
entire implementation or establish a release date by itself. Scope, remaining
funding, milestones, acceptance criteria, and delivery terms are agreed in
writing before payment. Unsolicited payment creates no delivery obligation.

## What feature sponsorship guarantees

After written scope, acceptance criteria, funding, and any delivery terms are
agreed, the work moves to the front of the feature queue. It is implemented
before community-voted and maintainer-selected feature work unless urgent
security, critical defects, or release-integrity work intervenes.

Sponsorship does not guarantee that an unreviewed idea will be accepted. It
does not buy a security exception, private fork in the public roadmap, control
of maintainership, or a merge that fails tests and review. If a proposal is not
feasible, no feature sponsorship is accepted for it.

## Recognition

Recognition is opt-in. Sponsor names or logos may appear on the website and
organization profile after permission and brand assets are provided. General
support can remain private.
