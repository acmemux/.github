# Sponsor AcmeMux

The public sponsorship page is
[acmemux.com/sponsor](https://acmemux.com/sponsor/). This repository copy keeps
the policy beside GitHub proposals and community health files.

AcmeMux is independent open-source work. Sponsorship helps pay for release
engineering, security review, compatibility qualification, provider upkeep,
documentation, infrastructure, and maintainer time.

## Monthly tiers

| Tier | Benefits |
| --- | --- |
| $5 Supporter | A sincere thank you. Recognition only; no roadmap, issue, or support priority. |
| $25 Sustaining supporter | A generous thank you. Recognition only; no roadmap, issue, or support priority. |
| $50 Company | Small website logo, Verified Sponsor badge artwork, and public sponsor listing. No response-time or resolution guarantee. |
| $500 Fast-track partner | Twelve-month commitment. Medium website logo, Verified Sponsor badge artwork, priority issue triage, direct maintainer email, and selection of which accepted roadmap feature enters the fast-track queue. |

Enrollment is direct while a public self-service funding account is being
established. Email
[sgurden@certleap.net](mailto:sgurden@certleap.net?subject=AcmeMux%20sponsorship)
to become a sponsor.

Sponsorship is not a support contract. Guaranteed response times, resolution
commitments, or implementation delivery require a separate enterprise
agreement priced for that obligation. Priority issue triage at the $500 tier is
not guaranteed resolution or 24-hour support.

## What fast-track sponsorship guarantees

After a 12-month commitment begins and the maintainer accepts the sponsor's
selection, that feature enters the fast-track queue ahead of community-voted
and maintainer-selected feature work. Urgent security, critical defects, and
release-integrity work can still intervene.

The selection must already be accepted as architecturally, legally, and
operationally feasible. Sponsorship does not buy a security exception, private
fork in the public roadmap, control of maintainership, or a merge that fails
tests and review.

## Recognition

Recognition is opt-in. Sponsor names or logos appear only after permission and
brand assets are provided. Any tier can remain private.
